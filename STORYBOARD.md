# Storyboard: Psyduck's Lakeside Daydream (可达鸭的湖畔奇遇)

**Logline**: Sleepy Psyduck dozes by a sunny pond until a cheeky dragonfly teases it from deadpan confusion into a joyful chase, an overwhelming headache that triggers glowing psychic levitation, and finally a cool sunset groove ending in its classic blank stare.

**World & Palette Arc**:
- **Setting**: Hand-painted lakeside bank with soft grass, swaying reeds, lily pads, distant misty hills, and warm paper grain.
- **Colour Arc**: Fresh sunlit morning sky (`#8EC3E6` / `#FFF5E2`) in Shots 1–2 → deepens into mysterious psychic twilight indigo/violet (`#2F3C7A` / `#7B5CA8`) at the headache/psychic climax in Shot 3 → blooms into warm golden-amber sunset (`#E8AA38` / `#F2A283`) in Shot 4.

**Motif**: A fluttering turquoise-winged **dragonfly** (`dragonfly`) that kicks off every reaction — teasing, leading the chase, spinning Psyduck dizzy, dancing on the beat, and sharing a quiet sunset moment.

**Psyduck's Emotion Arc**:
`sleepy` → `surprised` → `neutral` (blank stare) → `confused` → `idea` → `happy` → `excited` → `proud` → `laugh` → `dizzy` → `headache` (signature two-handed temple clutch) → `psychic` (levitation) → `relieved` → `cool` (shades) → `love` → `neutral` (deadpan blank-stare rhyme).
