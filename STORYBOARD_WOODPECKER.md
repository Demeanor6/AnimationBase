# Storyboard: Woodpecker's Forest Rhythm (啄木鸟的林间节拍)

**Logline**: Goofy-staring Woodpecker gets startled by a falling acorn in a sunlit clearing, hops eagerly to a tall oak tree for a rapid-fire pecking session until a shaken-loose pinecone knocks it dizzy, then laughs it off and grooves into a warm woodland dusk before returning to its signature blank stare.

**World & Palette Arc**:
- **Setting**: Hand-painted mossy woodland clearing with rolling pine hills, ferns, red-capped mushrooms, and a tall textured oak tree.
- **Colour Arc**: Fresh sunlit morning sky (`#8EC3E6` / `#FFF5E2`) in Shots 1–2 → bright golden sunbeams during the oak-pecking climax in Shot 3 → cozy amber woodland dusk (`#F0A868` / `#F9D89C`) with glowing fireflies in Shot 4.

**Motif**: Falling **acorns & pinecones** from the oak canopy — kicking off the adventure in Shot 1, paying off as comic slapstick in Shot 3, and glowing peacefully under dusk fireflies in Shot 4.

**Woodpecker's Emotion Arc**:
`neutral` (goofy stare) → `surprised` → `confused` → `idea` → `happy` → `excited` → `flutter` → `proud` → `determined` → `peck` → `dizzy` → `laugh` → `cool` (shades) → `love` → `neutral` (deadpan rhyme).

## Shots & Reads

### Shot 1 — `0.00–3.80s` · Morning Stare, Acorn Drop & Curiosity
- **Transition in**: `0.00–0.42s` `iris` opening onto Woodpecker (`u = 31`).
- **Event**: Woodpecker stands gazing blankly until an acorn drops beside it, sparking curiosity and a bright idea.
- **Reads**:
  - `0.20–0.90s` Woodpecker's iconic reference-image `neutral` stare with a cute blink.
  - `0.90–1.75s` Acorn bounces down beside it → `surprised` take (`!`).
  - `1.75–2.50s` `confused` (`?`) inspecting the acorn (`lookX / lookY`).
  - `2.50–3.50s` `idea` (`bulb`) → `happy` bounce ready to head for the oak.
- **Transition out**: `3.50–3.80s` forest-green `brushWipe`.

### Shot 2 — `3.80–8.00s` · Hop Walk, 360° Wing-Flutter Turnaround & Tree Lock-On
- **Transition in**: `3.80–4.10s` forest-green `brushWipe` clearing.
- **Event**: Woodpecker hops across the clearing toward the oak tree, performs a joyful 360° turnaround with a hovering wing flutter, then locks onto the oak trunk.
- **Reads**:
  - `4.05–5.20s` Side-view bouncy walk (`stroll` + `excited`) toward center clearing.
  - `5.20–6.25s` Full 360° drawn key-view turnaround with mid-air `flutter` wing flap.
  - `6.25–7.05s` Lands `proud` with sparkles.
  - `7.05–8.00s` Turns `side` toward the oak trunk in `determined` as camera tracks & pushes in.
- **Transition out**: Camera push carry-through (`zoom → 1.32`) into close action Shot 3.

### Shot 3 — `8.00–12.20s` · Rat-a-Tat Tree Pecking, Pinecone Bonk & Laugh
- **Transition in**: Cut on camera push motion into close-up by the oak trunk (`u = 31`).
- **Event**: Woodpecker unleashes rapid triple-tap pecks on the oak bark (sending wood chips flying) until a shaken-loose pinecone bonks its crest, turning it dizzy before bursting into laughter.
- **Reads**:
  - `8.10–9.70s` Rapid `peck` bursts striking the bark (`dx/rot` contact, flying wood chips, sparks, tree recoil).
  - `9.70–10.75s` Pinecone drops onto crest → turns front into `dizzy` (swirl eyes + stars).
  - `10.75–11.90s` Recovers into joyful `laugh` (`> <` eyes, open beak).
- **Transition out**: `11.90–12.20s` warm amber `brushWipe`.

### Shot 4 — `12.20–16.00s` · Dusk Shades Groove, Love & Goofy Stare Rhyme
- **Transition in**: `12.20–12.50s` amber `brushWipe` clearing.
- **Event**: At golden dusk with drifting fireflies, Woodpecker grooves in sunglasses (`cool`), melts into `love`, then deadpans back to its quintessential goofy stare before the iris shuts.
- **Reads**:
  - `12.40–13.60s` `cool` (`shades` + `music`) beat-locked dance under dusk fireflies.
  - `13.60–14.55s` `love` (heart eyes, rising hearts, wing wave).
  - `14.55–15.40s` Deadpan return to `neutral` goofy stare with blink (rhyming with Shot 1).
  - `15.35–16.00s` `iris` closes gently, holds `0.22s`, and shuts cleanly.
